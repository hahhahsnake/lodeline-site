One holding company, two working businesses.

Lodeline Holdings, LLC is a Texas company, formerly named Cochran Mineral Co., LLC. It owns and runs a precious-metals exploration and mining business in Nevada and a software studio that builds mobile apps and games.

Why the name

A lode is a vein of ore running through solid rock. Miners follow the line of it, and everything worthwhile branches from there. That's how we think of this company: one steady line, with each business we run branching off it.
